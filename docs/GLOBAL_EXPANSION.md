# Global Expansion Architecture

## Scope

BE Mastery prepares professional English communication across local and international career contexts. It provides educational guidance, not legal, immigration, licensing, wage, employment, or technical-qualification advice.

## Architecture Principle

One shared learning engine supports regional adaptation through versioned track and destination configuration. A destination changes context, examples, communication expectations, reviewed vocabulary, and interview preparation—not the core mission, evidence, privacy, or safety rules.

## Destination Contract

| Configuration domain | Adaptation requirement |
|---|---|
| Missions | Workplace settings, team norms, professional scenarios, learner goals |
| Conversations | Character framing, clarification norms, pace, directness, turn-taking expectations |
| Interviews | Question style, story structure, expectations around concise evidence and respectful questions |
| Vocabulary | Regionally appropriate, reviewed workplace language; avoid unsupported technical or regulatory claims |
| Culture guidance | Educational workplace communication guidance with source review and date/version |
| Career Center | Transparent “verify locally” prompts and official/employer-source signposting where available |

## Regional Starting Focus

| Region | Initial mission emphasis | Conversation and interview emphasis | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cameroon | Professional confidence, clear workplace English, multilingual-to-English transfer | Introductions, teamwork, supervisor updates, explaining experience honestly | Do not assume a single industry, language background, or credential path |
| Nigeria | Practical workplace fluency and career storytelling | Clear updates, collaboration, structured experience examples | Validate content with local users and reviewers |
| Ghana | Confident professional participation and teamwork | Clarification, meeting contribution, respectful follow-up | Avoid generalizing workplace culture |
| Kenya | Career mobility and clear professional communication | Concise experience stories, collaborative problem clarification | Use reviewed destination variants rather than stereotypes |
| South Africa | Cross-team clarity in diverse workplace contexts | Inclusive teamwork language, updates, safety/quality communication | Treat local requirements as external verification topics |
| Canada | Collaborative, concise, evidence-based communication | Structured behavioral interview answers, clarification, respectful contribution | No immigration, credential-recognition, or employment eligibility advice |
| USA | Clear self-advocacy and workplace storytelling | Direct but respectful interview answers, results and examples | No legal, labour, or employer-policy advice |
| UK | Clear, measured professional communication | Competency examples, polite clarification, concise updates | Avoid treating one communication style as universal |
| Australia | Practical, collegial workplace communication | Direct collaboration, safety/quality communication, experience examples | No visa, licensing, or site-specific safety advice |

## International Content Governance

1. A destination pack has an owner, market rationale, version, review date, and evidence source list.
2. A qualified local reviewer and target learners validate language, realism, accessibility, and harmful assumptions.
3. Technical profession content is reviewed for communication realism and safety boundary; it is not a substitute for trade standards or qualification.
4. Destination changes are tested for General English compatibility, privacy, accessibility, and prompt safety.
5. The product labels preparation as educational and directs time-sensitive requirements to current official/employer sources.

## Rollout Order

First prove the mission framework with General English and Welding. Then add destination variants for the existing tracks, beginning with user research in Cameroon and Nigeria before other African contexts. Add Canada, USA, UK, and Australia only with reviewed interview/culture packs and explicit compliance boundaries.
