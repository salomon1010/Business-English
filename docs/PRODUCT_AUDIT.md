# Product Audit — Vision 2 Baseline

## Verdict

BE Mastery has a credible shared learning engine and a strong local-first foundation. Its main product problem is not missing capability; it is fragmentation. A learner encounters curriculum, practice, coaching, simulations, progress, and career guidance as separate destinations instead of one career-building sequence.

## What Must Be Preserved

- One shared engine for General English and professional tracks.
- Local-first learner state and device-held recordings.
- General English's blue-violet identity and current curriculum.
- Welding's amber identity, professional scenario content, and voice-first conversation pattern.
- Evidence rule: no learner production means no performance score or claimed improvement.

## Ranked Issues

### Critical

1. The current journey has too many independently promoted starting points. A learner cannot always tell which action advances their professional goal.
2. Completion, inferred scores, and demonstrated spoken evidence need clear separation everywhere.
3. Simulation and coach outputs must be strictly grounded in the recorded/transcribed learner contribution; empty or interrupted work must remain unscored.
4. Practice, Shadow, Phrase Lab, Vocabulary, and simulation need one explicit mission context and a return path.

### Important

1. Profile and Progress repeat adaptive, readiness, and coaching information rather than assigning each surface one job.
2. Welding scenario catalogues are information-dense before a learner commits to a mission.
3. General English uses weeks while Welding uses stages; the shared engine needs a common mission vocabulary without renaming General English content prematurely.
4. Current local coach memory is useful but needs a compact, verified learner narrative rather than repeated competency prose.
5. Roadmap, changelog, and current product behavior have historical wording drift; Version 2 documents must be the product-direction reference.

### Nice to Have

1. Make evidence exportable as a learner-controlled portfolio summary after privacy and truthfulness controls are designed.
2. Add reviewed regional variants only after the core mission contract is proven.
3. Offer a mentor relationship view once character memory has an explicit consent and retention model.

## Success Criteria for the Redesign

A returning learner can identify their active track, current mission, next workplace action, evidence already captured, and next milestone in one screen. Every other feature is entered in service of that mission.
