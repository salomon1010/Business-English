# Professional Achievement System

## Philosophy

BE Mastery recognizes professional growth. It does not manufacture dopamine loops. Achievements are optional, private, evidence-led acknowledgements of meaningful communication moments.

## Achievement Levels

| Level | Meaning | Unlock condition |
|---|---|---|
| Started | Learner took a purposeful first step | Verified mission participation |
| Practised | Learner repeatedly trained a capability | Configured, evidenced practice threshold |
| Demonstrated | Learner addressed a stated objective in usable production | Objective-linked evidence |
| Milestone | Learner reached a career-relevant progression point | Multiple configured demonstrations/completions |
| Career Story | Learner can review an honest narrative of accumulated evidence | Learner-controlled summary of milestones |

## Initial Professional Achievements

| Achievement | Meaning | Evidence requirement |
|---|---|---|
| First Supervisor Conversation | Began a supervisor-focused communication mission | Completed relevant conversation step; production is labelled separately |
| First Safety Briefing | Practised a safety communication moment | Participation plus objective-linked evidence for higher level |
| First Inspection | Practised a quality/inspection communication moment | Completed relevant mission or observable response |
| First Toolbox Meeting | Practised a concise team briefing | Relevant recorded/transcribed production where available |
| First Successful Interview | Demonstrated configured interview objectives | Evidence must meet the configured objective rule; never inferred from opening a scenario |
| Career Ready | Reached a defined communication-readiness milestone | Must be described as a formative platform milestone, not job eligibility or certification |

## Unlockables

Unlockables are useful choices, not currency: a new mission scenario, another interviewer, an optional challenge prompt, an evidence review, or a career-story reflection. They never block basic learning, push purchase behavior, or imply professional authority.

## Career Story

The Career Story is a learner-controlled timeline of professional moments: mission completed, response produced, objective demonstrated, phrase or vocabulary used, coach focus, and milestone unlocked. It uses plain language: “Practised,” “Recorded,” “Demonstrated,” and “Next focus.” It does not convert local activity into an employment claim.

## Guardrails

- No public leaderboard or social comparison.
- No penalty for missed days; streaks are personal training history.
- No badge volume for its own sake.
- No achievement without a transparent event rule.
- No hidden scoring or fabricated celebration.
- Learners can review how an achievement was earned and remove local data using existing data controls.
