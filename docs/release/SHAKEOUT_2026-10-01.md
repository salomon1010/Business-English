# BE Mastery — staging shakeout / release-readiness audit

**Date:** 1 October 2026 · **Audited build:** `91a199a4` (`staging-with-polish-fix`),
the commit behind **staging.lomonec.com** at `be12-v609`.
**Production at the time:** `be12-v489`. **Fixes on:** `fix/ai-account-gate-honest-failures`.

**Nothing was deployed. Nothing was merged. No other session's work was touched.**

## What the audited code actually is

The staging snapshot differs from commit `91a199a4` in exactly two things, both
intended: `FB_CONFIG` points at the **be-mastery-test** validation project, and
`sw.js` carries `be12-v609` instead of `v598`. Everything else is byte-identical,
so auditing the commit is auditing staging.

## The owner's three reports, and what they actually were

| Reported | What was actually happening |
|---|---|
| "Shadow Studio translation sometimes does not work" | The learner was **not signed in**. The translation call is refused `401 auth_required`, and the app drew a generic error next to a Retry that could never succeed. |
| "The user's voice sometimes does not record" | **The recording was always fine.** The *transcription* was refused (401 signed out, 402 Premium-required on Free), and the turn reported "Nothing came through — tap to speak and try again", which reads as a dead microphone. |
| "Some functionality appears intermittent" | Not random. It varied with the learner's **state** — signed out / signed in Free / signed in Premium — and, on a device that had signed in before, with whether the session had finished restoring. |

One cause sits under all three: with `PREMIUM_ENFORCED=1`, **be-polish requires a
verified account on every route**, free ones included — `premiumGate`'s
`cap === null` branch still calls `capabilities()` so a call can be attributed.
"Free" means free of a *capability*, not of an *account*. The client did not know
this: `aiOff()` asked only about `POLISH_API`, `navigator.onLine` and `entLocked`.

## A. RELEASE BLOCKERS

**A1 — With Premium on, a Free learner cannot have any spoken answer heard. On Welding too.**
`ROUTE_CAP.transcribe = "ai_analysis"`, a paid capability, and `entGated()`
deliberately carries no track term since the owner's 30 September
one-subscription-covers-both-tracks decision. Measured: Welding + billing on →
`aiOff("ai_analysis")` true, **0 calls made**; billing off → transcribed normally.
So the Welding interview and workshop — the flagship spoken loop — ship unusable
for Free learners the moment billing goes on.
*Severity P0 · root cause: configuration / product decision · reproducible 20/20 ·
NOT fixed here — changing the entitlement matrix is the owner's call, not a defect
to patch silently.* Three ways out: make `transcribe` free for conversation turns;
or keep it Premium and say so **before** the learner starts a conversation rather
than at the end of a turn; or give Free a small daily allowance. The first is the
only one that keeps the Welding programme whole.

**A2 — Signed out plus Premium on means no AI anywhere.** Translation, IPA,
transcription, reports, the coach and TTS all come back 401. Production today is
unaffected (`PREMIUM_ENFORCED` off), so this blocks the **Premium launch**, not
today's build.
*Severity P0 · root cause: configuration + client gate · reproducible 20/20 ·
**FIXED** (the app now stops before the doomed call and asks the learner to sign
in) — but the product question of whether AI should need an account at all is
still the owner's.*

## B. CRITICAL DEFECTS

**B1 — A refused transcription was reported as a silent microphone.**
`fbTranscribe` returned `""` for a refusal (401/402/429/503) and for real silence
alike; `simRecordFinish` mapped `""` to `SIM_NOTHING_HEARD`. This is what sent the
owner looking at the microphone, and it hid the real cause completely.
*P1 · state management / UX · 20/20 · **FIXED**: the reason is recorded
(account / premium / offline / busy / server / restoring) and the turn reports it.
`SIM_NOTHING_HEARD` is kept for the case it actually describes.*

**B2 — The session-restore race made everything look intermittent.**
`fbLoad()` fetches three Firebase SDK files **in sequence** and `FBUser` is only
set when `onAuthStateChanged` first reports; the app is interactive well before
that, and no AI call site waited. A call in that window carried no token → 401.
*P1 · race condition · structurally certain, **not reproduced** in the harness,
which cannot hold a genuine persisted Firebase session — stated as unproven ·
**FIXED**: the signing fetch waits, bounded, for a token that is on its way, and
`fbAuthPending()` stops the app telling a signed-in learner to sign in, or a
paying one that their plan is too small, while the plan is unknowable.*

## C. IMPORTANT DEFECTS

**C1 — The whole workshop/interview voice surface is hard-coded English.**
`SIM_NOTHING_HEARD`, `'Listening'`, `'Processing your answer'`, `'Your turn — tap
the microphone to speak'`, `'Ready to listen'`, `'Voice recognition is unavailable
in this browser'`, `'Tap once to speak. Tap again when you finish.'` and the
microphone-blocked text all bypass `t()`. For a francophone-majority audience this
is the flagship feature speaking English.
*P2 · i18n · reproducible · **NOT fixed** — it is a self-contained piece of work
(≈8 strings × 16 languages) and deliberately out of scope here. My new messages
match the surrounding English on purpose rather than leaving one translated line
in six English ones.*

**C2 — A failed entitlement check keeps showing the old plan.** `entRefresh`'s
`catch` returns `entView()`, so a network or CORS failure leaves a cached
**Premium / Active** on screen. This is how "the frontend displays Premium" and "the
request 401s" were true at once. Display-only and documented as intentional; the
server still decides every spend. Worth a one-line "could not confirm your plan"
rather than silent confidence.
*P2 · by design · not fixed.*

**C3 — The test harness can certify the wrong code.** Many suites spawn their own
server on a **hardcoded** port and ignore `BASE`; `spawn(..., stdio:"ignore")` fails
silently when the port is taken, and another session's worktree keeps answering.
During this audit that produced **two false smoke failures**, and
`premium-boundary` reporting **44/47** with `homeRowsPremHTML` apparently
"missing" — on a verified free port the same file is **47/47**. Separately, a
runner using `timeout` (absent on macOS) printed zero failures for 30 suites.
*P1 for release confidence · tooling · reproducible · **not fixed in code** — any
prior "all green" taken on a shared port should be re-run on a verified free port
before it is trusted.*

**C4 — "How it works" on the Practice Partner card is unreachable.** `ppHowSheet()`
is defined, `.pp-how-link` is styled and `.pp-how-ov` is built — but **nothing
emits the link**, so there is no way into the sheet. On
`feature/practice-partner` the Practice-tab card carries it:
`<span class="pp-how-link" role="button" … onclick="…ppHowSheet()">`. That span is
absent from this lineage and from `origin/main`, so it was lost on the way across;
the sheet, its CSS and its overlay are all dead code behind it. CLAUDE.md still
describes the feature as present.
*P2 · data/merge loss · reproducible · **not fixed** — restoring markup from
another branch is a merge decision, not a shakeout fix. One line brings it back.*

**C5 — Practice Partner has effectively not been tested locally.**
`tests/partner.mjs`, `live-rounds.mjs` and `hidden-switch.mjs` all **`SKIP` and
exit 0** when the dev Worker is not reachable on 127.0.0.1:8787 — so a normal run
reports no failures while testing nothing. When I started the Worker, the local D1
had **no tables at all** (`D1_ERROR: no such table: members`): the migrations had
never been applied to the dev database, which is what a "consent sheet" failure
looks like from the outside. After `wrangler d1 migrations apply be-partner
--local --env dev`, the suites run. C4 above is what the first of them found
immediately — a defect that had been sitting behind the skip.
*P1 for release confidence · tooling / environment · reproducible · the skip is a
deliberate design but it reads as a pass in any summary.*

## D. COSMETIC / MINOR

**D1** `simRecordFinish` discards any clip under 1,200 bytes without comment, so a
very short real answer is indistinguishable from silence. Not observed to fire;
worth a look if "nothing came through" persists after the fixes.

## H. PRODUCTION RISKS

1. **Switching `PREMIUM_ENFORCED` on in production is not a flag flip.** It makes
   an account mandatory for every AI route and makes `ai_analysis` paid. Without
   A1 settled, that silently removes spoken practice from every Free learner,
   Welding included.
2. **A cached old client keeps the old behaviour.** A learner on a pre-fix
   `index.html` after enforcement goes on sees the generic error and the
   "Nothing came through" message — the signing wrapper's comment already notes a
   cached client is a real case. Order matters: client first, enforcement second.
3. **Green test runs may be someone else's code** (C3). Re-verify before release.
4. **`boot-recovery` is 3/7** on this snapshot, consistent with the known
   pre-existing state; the failures are curriculum-recovery and FAB placement, not
   anything this audit changed. Worth closing before submission rather than
   carrying four known-failing boot checks into a store review.

## I. RECOMMENDED NEXT ACTIONS

1. **Decide A1.** Should hearing a spoken answer be Premium? My recommendation:
   make `transcribe` free for conversation turns and keep the *analysis* and the
   written report paid. The practice itself is what the app promises, and the code
   already says "the practice itself is never metered".
2. Settle whether AI should require an account at all (A2), or accept the
   sign-in prompt the fix now shows.
3. Translate the workshop/interview voice surface (C1) — French first.
4. Re-run the whole suite on verified free ports and close `boot-recovery` (C3, H4).
   Make the three partner suites **fail loudly** rather than `SKIP` in a release
   run, and document `wrangler d1 migrations apply be-partner --local --env dev`
   as part of setting the dev Worker up (C5).
5. Restore the one-line `pp-how-link` span from `feature/practice-partner`, or
   delete the sheet, its CSS and its overlay — one or the other (C4).
6. Restage with these fixes and repeat the manual pass, then do the **real-iPhone**
   audio pass (G) before any Apple submission.
7. Ship the client before enforcement (H2).
