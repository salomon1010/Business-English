feat(android): the home-screen widget for the Play app, fed by a tiny Worker

Owner, 5 Oct 2026: "can you create for android version?" — the iOS widget's
twin for the Play app: the same three sizes, the streak ring whose flame is
the day's mood, today's step by name with its button, the road-map strip and
the phases, the words due, this week's goal, the best streak; the same deep
links; the programme's colours and the app's theme.

How the data gets there — the one real difference from iOS. The Play app is
a Trusted Web Activity: the page runs inside Chrome and the TWA library
keeps the browser session private, so there is no supported page→app
channel on the phone (android-browser-helper #472). So:
- the app mints a random 32-hex widget id and appends ?wid= to every launch
  URL (BEWidgetLaunch.decorate, Chrome's "query parameters" pattern);
- index.html captures it beside the ?flags= strip (localStorage
  be_widget_wid; ?widget=<act> → the page a widget tap asked for, honoured
  by homeDeepLink) and publishes the SAME snapshot the iOS widget gets to
  be-widget (widgetFeedSend: debounced, changed-only, ≤1/min, forced at boot
  and on hide; sign-out DELETEs it);
- be-widget (backend/widget/: D1, POST/GET/DELETE /feed, shapeSnap
  allow-list so nothing personal can be stored, 60 writes/h, 30-day rows,
  no cron) — be-widget-staging DEPLOYED (D1 91355285-…); production is the
  owner's step (docs/ANDROID_WIDGET.md §4), WIDGET_API is empty until then;
- BEWidgetProvider pulls it every 30 min, 25 s after a launch and 4 s after
  the learner comes back (LauncherActivity hooks), caches it, draws
  RemoteViews with the ring / strip / phase bars painted as bitmaps.

Bubblewrap overwrites hand edits on every `update`, so the widget lives in
playstore/android-widget/ and apply.mjs copies and patches it into the
generated project (idempotent; refuses a foreign LauncherActivity).
Verified: a fresh Bubblewrap 1.25.0 project + apply + gradle assembleDebug
compiles; the debug preview screen on the API 35 emulator draws all three
sizes in dark/light, General English/Welding, done/at-risk. A plain View as
a spacer crashed RemoteViews inflation — FrameLayout now.
