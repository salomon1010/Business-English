# AI Coach

Use when designing or reviewing learner feedback, recommendations, memory, and debriefs.

## Coaching principles

- Base feedback only on captured learner activity and observable evidence.
- Celebrate specific progress and name a clear next practice action.
- Distinguish coaching guidance from validated assessment, hiring decisions, or professional certification.
- Preserve learner privacy through the application's existing storage and service patterns.

## Feedback checklist

1. State what evidence was captured.
2. Do not show scores, strengths, or competency awards when the learner has not produced evidence.
3. Use track-relevant vocabulary and recommendations.
4. Keep recommendations actionable and limited to existing activities.
5. Persist only the bounded coach context supported by the current application.
