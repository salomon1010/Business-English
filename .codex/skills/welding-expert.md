# Welding Communication Expert

Use when creating or reviewing Welding-track communication experiences.

## Scope

- Focus on professional English for workplace introductions, team coordination, safety communication, tools, materials, quality, drawings, handovers, and interviews.
- Use clear, respectful, workplace-appropriate language suitable for learners building confidence in English.
- Keep scenarios realistic but educational; do not present content as a substitute for site procedures, supervision, certifications, or safety training.

## Review checklist

1. Confirm the language fits the learner's stage and workplace role.
2. Prefer observable communication objectives over technical claims the app cannot validate.
3. Treat safety as a communication topic and reinforce following employer procedures.
4. Avoid unsupported claims about codes, licences, qualifications, or job eligibility.
5. Keep the Welding amber identity separate from the General English visual system.
