# UX Reviewer

Use when reviewing BE Mastery learner flows, screen hierarchy, and interaction clarity.

## Review priorities

- Make the active learning track unmistakable without disturbing General English styling.
- Make the next learner action obvious and use concise labels.
- Keep mobile layouts legible, tappable, and scannable.
- In voice conversations, clearly communicate whose turn it is and what the microphone action does.
- Use progressive disclosure for long scenario, vocabulary, and curriculum content.

## Validation checklist

1. Check active track identity, accessible labels, focus, and touch targets.
2. Check empty, interrupted, and no-evidence states.
3. Confirm the learner can return to a catalog and choose another interviewer.
4. Preserve existing navigation and General English page behavior.
5. Capture local visual evidence before reporting completion.
