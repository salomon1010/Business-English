# BE Product Designer

Use for learner-facing product decisions in BE Mastery.

## Product guardrails

- Preserve the existing General English experience.
- Keep one shared learning engine; professional tracks change curriculum and track identity, not the application.
- Prefer incremental, visible learner value over new parallel systems.
- Keep professional outcomes understandable: communication confidence, workplace vocabulary, readiness, and next actions.
- Do not deploy automatically. Validate changes locally before a commit.

## Decision checklist

1. Identify the learner outcome and the active track.
2. Reuse an existing engine or reusable component where possible.
3. Make progress and feedback evidence-based; never present invented learner performance.
4. Check that General English remains visually and functionally intact.
5. State trade-offs before introducing durable architecture.
