1Getting started & onboarding

The app is a complete 12-week training program for professional spoken English: 6 days a week, 25 focused minutes a day, with instant feedback on every recording. Everything is saved automatically on your device.

  1. Open the app in Chrome or Edge (best) — https://app.lomonec.com/. On Android, use the browser menu → Add to Home Screen to install it like a real app (it works offline).
  2. Complete the 60-second welcome: your name → what you do → your #1 goal → your daily practice slot → say one sentence aloud for your baseline score. The whole app personalizes itself around your answers.
  3. Allow microphone access when asked — the recorder and speaking feedback need it.
  4. You land in the 🎯 Shadow tab — pick a starter video and do your first 30-second sprint. That's day 1 done.
💡 The golden rule: every session must produce evidence — a recording, a transcript, saved words, or correction notes. If you didn't record or write anything, it wasn't a real session.

5A daily 25-minute session

One page holds your whole workout: timer, minute-by-minute template, recorder with live transcription, your script, notes and self-score.

  1. Press ▶ Start on the 25-minute timer and follow the timed template steps, ticking each one off.
  2. Complete "🎬 My example — what I plan to say" — the box now opens pre-filled with a starter for that exact day. On Tuesdays, for example, it lays out the labelled prompts Current role: · Core responsibilities: · Previous experience: · Current focus:; other days seed it with the week's phrases. Finish the sentences in your own words — this is the target your recording is scored against. (On shadowing and phrase days the box starts collapsed — tap it open if you want to jot a script.)
  3. Press 🎙️ and speak. While you record, the app transcribes you. Aim for 20–60 seconds of real speech — one-second clips can't be analyzed.
  4. Stop → the full feedback report appears (see Section 7): word-by-word score, grammar, vocabulary, pace. Your words also fill "🎙️ My transcript" automatically.
  5. Record a second take — the 📈 waveform shows your new voice layered over the previous one, with a pacing verdict like "4.5s tighter — more confident pacing 📈".
  6. Write your top corrections in notes, self-score 1–10, and ✅ Mark session complete.

Two tabs: the plan and the doing

The session splits in two, with the timer and this week’s phrases visible above both. 25-minute template is the timed checklist you follow; Practise & record holds your example, the recorder, your transcript, notes and self-score. Switching between them never disturbs a running timer or a recording in progress.

📌 Example — Week 1 Tuesday, "Tell me about yourself"

My example:

"I currently work as a data engineer. My focus is on the reporting pipeline. I'm responsible for data quality."

You record it, and the report shows: 95% — one yellow word (responsible), a grammar catch ("I am agree" → "I agree"), pace 132 wpm ✅. You drill the yellow word with 🔊/🎤, record take 2, and the attempt line reads 82% → 95% 📈.

6🎯 Shadowing Studio — the practice lab

Shadowing = speaking along with a native speaker to copy their rhythm, stress and melody. The Studio makes it effortless: pick a clip from the built-in library (transcript included) or paste any YouTube link, mark a 20–30 second segment, and loop it while you shadow.

Cut and loop a clip

  1. Pick a video: choose a clip from the 🎬 library below (Jobs, MLK, Michelle Obama, TED talks and many more — each shows a “Captions ready” badge), or paste any YouTube link → ▶ Load video.
  2. Play, then ⬇ Mark start. A live counter appears and counts the seconds — at 20s it turns green ("you can mark the end now ✅"), at 30s it pulses gold.
  3. ⬇ Mark end → the clip now loops automatically 🔁. During playback the counter shows your position: 12s / 30s, resetting to 0 every loop.
  4. The transcript fills in for you. For library clips, the moment you Mark end, the app pulls the captions for exactly that segment into the “🎬 Clip transcript & notes” box, already stress-marked (CAPS = stressed parts). Tap “📄 Get transcript” to re-pull after changing your marks, or “✕ Clear” to start a fresh clip.
  5. Navigate inside the clip with ⟲ (restart), ↺5 / 5↻ (jump 5s back/forward — clicks stack), the speed button (1× → .75× → .5×) and 🔁 loop on/off.
  6. ⭐ Save clip — saved clips reload with one tap, with your marks, transcript and recordings intact. A refresh also restores your last clip automatically.

🧍 Posture Coach — executive presence while you speak

  1. Click "🧍 Posture coach" under the recorder (Shadow or any session page) and allow the camera.
  2. Sit upright for 3 seconds — the coach calibrates to your upright posture.
  3. Practice normally. A small glowing skeleton avatar (never your video) mirrors you: green = executive posture · yellow = head tilted, raise the phone · orange glow = slouching, shoulders back.
  4. In a dark room, the screen automatically becomes a 💡 ring light to illuminate your face (one tap to turn off). If tracking can't see you, it pauses gracefully and lets you focus on voice.
  5. Stop the coach to get your session result — e.g. "🧍 88% upright" — with advice.
🔒 Zero-capture privacy: camera frames are analyzed on your device and discarded instantly. No video or photo is ever stored or uploaded — only anonymous numeric posture coordinates exist, and they never leave your device.

Shadow and record

  1. Shadow the loop 6–8×: listen → repeat in the gaps → speak with the audio → speak alone.
  2. Check the 🎬 clip transcript. For library clips it's already filled from your marks and stress-marked; for a pasted video, type what the speaker says and tap “✨ Mark stress”. STRESSED parts appear in CAPS, pauses marked with /.
  3. Press 🎙️ and shadow one full take. On stop: your words appear in 🎙️ My transcript, and the full speaking-feedback report is generated from that exact recording.
  4. Repeat the clip — every take is an attempt: "This clip · attempt 3 — 80% → 95% → 100% 📈 you're improving!"

8📚 Vocabulary, trouble words & quiz

  1. Key vocabulary appears in every feedback report — words from your own speech with level badges (A2…C1). Tap a word for its dictionary definition, IPA and real human audio; use 🎤 Say it to test yourself; 🔖 save the good ones (or "Save all words").
  2. ⬆️ Level up your words: the app spots casual words you used and suggests the executive upgrade — you said "get" → try "obtain" — with audio and save buttons.
  3. 💪 Trouble words (Shadow → Trouble words tab): every word the feedback flags collects here automatically. Drill each with 🔊 / 🐢 / 🎤 — pronounce it correctly as many times as you missed it and it's cleared with a 🏆.
  4. Flashcards, quizzes and crosswords: your saved words become a deck. The quiz reads you a word's meaning — you must say the word out loud; the app judges both recall and pronunciation.

✏️ Grammar exercises

Seven categories of business-English drill: fill in the blanks · sentence correction · verb tenses · articles · prepositions · business grammar · weekly grammar review. Eight multiple-choice questions per run, and every answer shows the reason behind it — so you learn the rule, not just the answer. Your best score per category is kept.

📌 Example — the quiz

"What word is this? — The people with an interest or concern in a business project."

You press 🎤 and say "stakeholders" → "🏆 correct AND well pronounced!"

9Phrases & 💼 Executive Polish

💼 Executive Polish — say it like a leader

  1. Type it, or tap the 🎤 mic and speak. The mic is yours to control: tap once to start, say a full sentence or a whole paragraph (it keeps listening through your pauses), then tap again to stop. Your professional versions appear straight away.
  2. Pick from two boardroom-ready versions. Each one rephrases what you said in confident, professional English and notes the idiom or key phrase it uses — so you pick up new language, not just a single answer.
  3. Not quite right? Press ✨ Polish it again for two fresh versions, as many times as you like — the words and idioms change each time.
  4. 🔊 Hear it, 🐢 slow it, 📋 copy it for the version you like. Then 🗑 Clear the box to start a new sentence.
📌 Example

"before starting our project I need us to verify all the requirements"

→ "Before we kick off the project, we need to sign off on all the requirements."

Executive Polish writes these versions with AI, so it needs an internet connection. Offline, it falls back to a quick clean-up (removing fillers and hedging and fixing obvious grammar).

Two tabs: phrases and idioms

The Phrase Lab separates Professional phrases (everyday sentence-starters) from Business idioms, so you can work on one kind at a time. The week chips (W1–W12) still filter within whichever tab you’re on.

The phrase bank

10–13 phrases and idioms per week, matched to the theme. For each: say it 5×, 🎙️ record your own sentence, and Mark mastered when it's naturally yours. Sunday's job: pick 5 to use in real meetings next week.

10Reviews & 📈 Progress Studio

  1. 📖 Your story this week — an auto-written summary of your practice: days, words spoken, score trend, new vocabulary, trouble words to clear.
  2. The four charts — 🎯 pronunciation score journey · 📚 vocabulary growth · ⚡ speaking-speed gauge (Warming up / Just right / Fast) · 🗓️ words spoken per day. All measured automatically.
  3. Every Sunday: listen to 2 recordings, move the five 1–10 sliders (clarity, fluency, pronunciation, business naturalness, confidence), write 3 wins / 3 weaknesses / 3 corrections, and complete the review.
  4. Weeks 4, 8, 12: fill the Month Dashboard — the big checkpoint that sets the next phase.

📅 Your Progress Calendar

  1. This week: a weekly-goal counter, e.g. 4/6 days.
  2. Month heat-map: each practised day glows brighter the more you did that day — tap any day to see its detail.

11🛠️ Professional tracks — English for your trade

Everything above teaches general professional English. A professional track keeps the same 12-week engine and swaps the content for the job you actually do: your workshops, your vocabulary, the standards you are held to, and interviews with the people you would really meet.

  1. Turn it on: 👤 Profile → App Setup → Professional track, then choose Welding. Choose General English in the same place to switch back — nothing you have already done is lost either way.
  2. Name your trade: in the Career Center under Your profession, pick Welder, Pipefitter or Boilermaker. This is not a label. It changes the scenario you are given, the vocabulary your answers are checked for, the model answers you are shown and the codes you are assessed against.
  3. The chip in the header shows the track and trade you are currently being assessed as, on every screen. Tap it to open the Career Center from anywhere.

🌍 The Career Center

Your professional home. Open it from the header chip or from the row on the Dashboard.

  1. Career readiness — three figures: career readiness, interview readiness, and the number of spoken answers recorded. They are counted from answers you actually said and the app scored. Opening an activity never moves them.
  2. Professional Interview Coaches — the interview for your current stage is shown first; the other eleven fold underneath.
  3. Career Gap Analysis — your highest-value growth areas, taken from your Passport and history.
  4. Resume & LinkedIn Coach — write a short professional summary and get concise rewrites back.
  5. Destination interview guidance — what your chosen destination expects, its workplace culture, and the certification roadmap. Tap to unfold.

🎤 Interview coaches — and what the report tells you

Twelve interviewers, each rehearsing one part of your professional story: your introduction, a shift handover, a stop-work conversation, a quality report, permit and isolation, and so on. Choose any of them, in any order, as often as you like. You speak your answers out loud.

  1. During: your talking points tick off as you cover them, and the interviewer steers towards the ones you have not.
  2. After — Practice summary: how many talking points you covered, and which.
  3. Your performance analysis opens the real report. It has two halves.
  4. What your answer covered — measured against what a competent answer to that question contains. It names what you covered, what you missed, and the words that would have carried it. Then it shows a strong answer in full, with a button to hear it and say it back.
  5. Word by word — every sentence you recorded, scored per word from your own audio, with the weak words collected so you can drill them in Practice.

Say enough to be assessed. A one-word answer is reported honestly as "not enough was said to assess this interview" — the app will not invent a score, or praise, from nothing.

The model answers show you how to say what you checked and what you found. They are language models, not trade instruction: they never tell you what to check, what to set, or whether work is safe to start. Those come from your procedure, your supervisor and your qualification.

👷 Professional Workplace Scenarios

Under 🎮 Practice, a full workplace conversation rather than a one-to-one interview. A team is in the room with you — Maya from HR, Daniel the supervisor, Luis on tools, Amelia on materials and quality, Priya on safety — and whoever is speaking appears at the top, so you always know who has the floor. Speak, and the person who answers is the one who would answer on a real site.

🎫 Your Professional Skills Passport

On the 👤 Profile tab. It leads with evidence: workshops practised, answers recorded, and how many of them covered what a competent answer needs. The practice-activity bars below it are a fair record of effort — they are counts of what you have done, not a measure of your trade ability. Nothing on this page is evidence of certification or eligibility to work; those are proved by test certificates and the awarding body.

📶 It still works on a bad connection

If the network drops mid-interview, the interviewer does not stop. It works down its question list instead, and tells you once that it has done so. Your talking points still tick off from what you actually say. Everything else — the plan, the phrase bank, your recordings, the calendar — has always worked offline.

12👤 Account, backup & privacy

  1. Profile: edit your name, role, goal and daily slot anytime — the whole app re-personalizes.
  2. Sync across your devices: optional and free. Create an account and your progress, streak, scores, vocabulary and reviews follow you from phone to laptop. Skip it and everything still works on this device.
  3. Plan: the Free plan includes everything. Premium is coming later — deeper AI coaching, AI conversation practice, a pronunciation coach, and team dashboards.
  4. Backup: click Export JSON weekly and keep the file safe; Import restores it on any device.
  5. Privacy: progress, recordings and transcripts live on your device. Only if you sign in does your progress sync to your account — your recordings never leave the device either way.
⛔ "Reset everything" permanently erases all progress and recordings on this device. Export a backup first.

Daily reminder

In Settings you can switch on a daily reminder and pick the time. If the app is open at that moment your phone shows a notification; if it isn't, the app tells you the next time you open it that the day's session is still waiting. It stays quiet once you've practised, and nudges at most once a day.

Nothing is deleted without asking

Every destructive action — deleting a recording or a saved clip, clearing a clip, importing progress, resetting, signing out — shows a confirmation first, naming exactly what is about to go. Cancel is the default, so a stray tap can’t lose your work.

After ten completed sessions with no account and no backup, the app will ask once whether you’d like to protect your progress. Browser storage really can disappear — clearing browsing data, or iOS reclaiming space from an app you haven’t opened in a while — so a backup or sync is worth having.

13🔗 Sharing, your certificate & telling others

📊 Your progress card

Tap Create progress card and the app draws an image of where you have got to — week, sessions completed, streak. On a phone your normal share sheet opens, so it can go straight to WhatsApp, LinkedIn or a message; on a desktop the image downloads instead. The app’s address travels with it, so anyone who sees the card can find their way here. You need a few sessions behind you before the card unlocks — it should show something worth showing.

🏆 Your certificate

Finish all 84 sessions and the certificate unlocks on the same page, carrying your name and the date. Until then the card shows how many of the 84 you have done. There is a Share on LinkedIn button next to it: LinkedIn cannot accept an image from a link, so the file saves first and the post composer opens — attach the image you have just saved.

🔗 Bring someone with you

Share the app sends a plain invitation and the address, with no progress attached — the right thing for a colleague who is holding the same words back. Where the share sheet is unavailable the link is copied to your clipboard instead.

⭐ Rating the app

After seven completed sessions a card appears on your Dashboard asking for a Google Play rating. It is how the next person finds the app. Not now hides it for a month, and rating it once removes it for good — it will never nag you daily.

Nothing is shared automatically. No card, certificate or invitation ever leaves your device unless you tap the button yourself.

15FAQ & troubleshooting

The microphone / speaking feedback doesn't work

Use Chrome or Edge — speech recognition needs their engine (Safari partially works; Firefox doesn't). Click the 🔒 icon in the address bar → Microphone → Allow → reload. On macOS also check System Settings → Privacy → Microphone.

I refreshed and my clip/recordings seemed gone

Fixed — the Shadow tab now restores your last clip, marks, transcript and recordings automatically after a refresh. Recordings always survive refreshes; they live in your browser's database.

My progress disappeared

You probably opened a different browser or a private window. Return to your usual browser, or restore your JSON backup (👤 Account). This is why the weekly export habit matters.

My recording says 0:00 / can't be analyzed

It was too short. Speak for at least 20 seconds — feedback quality grows with every sentence you say. (Fresh recordings can display "0:00" until you press play — that's a browser quirk, not data loss.)

Can I install it as an app?

Yes — on Android Chrome: menu ⋮ → Add to Home Screen / Install app. It opens full-screen with the bottom navigation and works offline.

Does the Posture Coach record me?

No. The camera feed is analyzed frame-by-frame on your device and each frame is immediately discarded. No video or image is ever saved or transmitted — only anonymous posture coordinates (numbers) exist while the coach runs. Turning the coach off releases the camera completely.

Does my voice go to a server?

Recordings stay on your device. The speech engine used for transcription and dictation is your browser's own (Chrome may process audio on Google's servers as part of its speech service). Executive Polish sends only the text you typed or dictated to write its professional versions. Dictionary definitions are fetched anonymously. Your recordings never leave your device.

In what order should I do the weeks?

In order, 1 → 12. Phases build on each other, and weeks 4, 8, 12 end with month dashboards.
