*Say it in the app first. Then say it on site.*

Five minutes before the toolbox talk you already know what you have to report. The joints you finished, the fan that was not pulling properly, the root pass you are starting today. You know the work. The problem is the English it comes out in.

Open BE Mastery → choose *Welding* → *Phrase Lab* → *Executive Polish*. Say it out loud into the app, or type it. Then tap Polish it.

Real example, straight from the app:

_What you were going to say:_
"Yesterday I finished the joints on line 3, but the extraction fan was not working well, so I stopped and told the foreman. Today I will do the root pass on the new spool, and tomorrow I will grind and check the welds."

_What you say instead:_
"I wrapped up the joints on line 3 yesterday; however, the extraction fan was underperforming, so I halted work and informed the foreman. Today, I plan to tackle the root pass on the new spool, and tomorrow I'll grind and inspect the welds."

Same facts. Same job. But now it names the problem, says the stop-work out loud, and sounds like a tradesman the supervisor trusts with the next job. And you keep the words: *wrapped up*, *underperforming*, *halted work and informed the foreman*.

*The habit*
1. In the van or the changing room, say the report you are about to give.
2. Polish it. Two site-ready versions come back, each naming the phrase that made it stronger.
3. Tap *Hear it*, then *Slow*. Say it back until it comes out clean through a helmet.
4. Say that version at the talk.

Use it before the toolbox talk, the shift handover, raising a hazard, talking to QA, asking the supervisor for what you need — and before the interview that gets you the job abroad.

Do it every morning and your trade English grows by two or three real phrases a day. Not from a word list — from the sentences you actually had to say.
