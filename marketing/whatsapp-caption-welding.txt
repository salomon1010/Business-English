*You know the work. Now say it in English.*

You can weld it. You can read the drawing. Then the interview starts, the words come out small — and they hear a beginner.

BE Mastery gives you twelve interviewers who work where you work: HR, your supervisor, the tool room, materials, safety. You answer out loud. Afterwards it tells you what a strong answer needed — what you covered, what you missed, and the exact sentence you should have said, so you can hear it and say it back.

What's inside:
• 12 interviews — introduction, shift handover, stop-work, quality report, permit and isolation
• A report that names the gap, not just a score
• 12 workshop scenarios with the whole crew in the room
• Welder, pipefitter or boilermaker — the words and the model answers change with you
• Every word scored from your own recording
• Keeps going when the network drops on site

Say one word and it tells you plainly there wasn't enough to assess. Nothing here invents a score.

Free to start. No ads. Instructions in 15 languages. It opens in your browser — nothing to install.
