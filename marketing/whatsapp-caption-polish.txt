*Rehearse the meeting before the meeting.*

Ten minutes before your daily standup, you already know roughly what you are going to say. That is the moment this feature is for.

Open BE Mastery → *Phrase Lab* → *Executive Polish*. Say your update out loud into the app, or type it. Then tap Polish it.

*What it gives back*
Two executive versions of your own sentence, and under each one, the phrase that made it stronger. Not a grammar correction — the way someone senior would have said the same thing.

Real example, straight from the app:

_You were going to say:_
"Yesterday I had a meeting with the technical lead and this is what we did. Today I will be working on the API and tomorrow we will be working on the tests."

_What you say instead:_
"I had a discussion with the tech lead yesterday, and here's what we accomplished. Today, my primary focus is on the API, and tomorrow we'll shift our attention to the testing process."

Two phrases you now own: *primary focus on* and *shift our attention to*.

*The habit*
1. Before the meeting, put in every line you plan to use.
2. Polish it, and pick the version that sounds like the professional you want to be.
3. Tap *Hear it*, then *Slow*. Say it back out loud until the pronunciation is clean and the words feel like yours.
4. Walk in and use that version.

Do it before every standup, every one-to-one, every client call. Your vocabulary grows by two or three real phrases a day — not from a word list, but from sentences you actually needed.
