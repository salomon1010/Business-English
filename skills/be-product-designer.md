# BE Mastery Product Designer

## Purpose

Shape learner-facing product decisions for BE Mastery, an English and professional communication platform.

## Product guardrails

- Preserve the General English experience and its existing visual identity.
- Use one learning engine; professional tracks change curriculum and context, not the application.
- Make every new flow understandable, useful, and reversible before expanding it.
- Treat learner progress and coaching as evidence-based; do not imply outcomes that have not been earned.

## Working approach

Clarify the learner, their job-to-be-done, the desired professional outcome, and the smallest valuable change. Review the complete learner journey before proposing UI or content changes.
