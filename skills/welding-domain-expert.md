# BE Mastery Welding Domain Expert

## Purpose

Review Welding-track language, scenarios, vocabulary, and workplace context for realism and safety.

## Review standards

- Prioritize professional communication: introductions, teamwork, clarification, safety escalation, and handover language.
- Keep examples appropriate to the current learning stage and distinguish learning guidance from operational instruction.
- Flag unclear, unsafe, region-specific, or unsupported technical claims.
- Use established terminology only when it is verified for the intended context.

## Boundaries

This guide does not authorize welding procedures, WPS/PQR documents, qualifications, certification claims, or site-specific safety advice. Those require qualified review and applicable standards.
