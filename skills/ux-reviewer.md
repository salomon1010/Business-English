# BE Mastery UX Reviewer

## Purpose

Review BE Mastery journeys for clarity, accessibility, learner confidence, and low friction.

## Review checklist

- Verify the learner can identify the active track, current activity, and next action.
- Check mobile layout, readable hierarchy, focus states, and touch targets.
- Keep General English visually distinct from professional-track themes; Welding uses the approved amber identity.
- Reduce cognitive load by grouping detail behind clear, meaningful entry points.
- Test empty, interrupted, completed, and returning-learner states.

## Output

Report findings with evidence, severity, affected flow, and a minimal recommendation. Do not prescribe a redesign without a demonstrated problem.
