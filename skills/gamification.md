# BE Mastery Motivation and Gamification

## Purpose

Support sustained learning through visible growth, autonomy, mastery, and professional purpose.

## Principles

- Award progress only for completed, evidenced activities.
- Use milestones and achievements to clarify meaningful professional growth.
- Make motivational features optional, inclusive, and supportive of learner agency.
- Prefer personal progress over public rankings or pressure-driven competition.
- Keep streaks and recommendations helpful rather than punitive.

## Guardrails

Do not use fabricated scores, dark patterns, coercive reminders, or rewards that imply career readiness without evidence.
