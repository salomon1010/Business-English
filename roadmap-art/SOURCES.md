Photos behind the progress ring on the Road map card (`rmLook()` in index.html).

- `welding.jpg` and `general.jpg` (514×274) are cropped from the owner's own
  reference mock-ups for the Road map redesign, supplied on 30 Sep 2026. The

- The owner described those mock-ups as generated reference images, so the

  under which plan, is not recorded here. Check that before any use in store
  marketing (see `rp-photos/SOURCES.md` for the same open question).
