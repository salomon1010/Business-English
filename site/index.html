01 The problem
Knowing English isn't the same as speaking confidently.

You read it fine. You follow the meeting. Then it's your turn, and the sentence you had ready arrives a beat too late — so you say the shorter, smaller version instead.

That gap isn't knowledge. It's rehearsal. It closes the same way it closes for musicians and athletes: a little, out loud, every day.

02 The method

Twenty-five minutes. Every day.

One guided session a day for twelve weeks, in four short steps. You don't choose what to study. The road map already knows. You just show up and speak.

Listen

Today's phrases, or a real speaker, and the situation they belong to.

Practise aloud

Shadow it until the rhythm stops feeling foreign.

Record

Say it in your own voice. This is the part that counts.

Read the report

How you came across, what to fix, your sentences rebuilt.

04 Shadow Studio

Borrow the rhythm of someone who already sounds right.

Pick a real talk, interview or lesson. Watch it with the words lighting up, shadow it one paragraph at a time, then say it without looking. When you do, it's yours.

  • Watch — the video stays on top and the transcript runs underneath in short paragraphs, with the word being said lit up.
  • Shadow — the clip loops on one paragraph. Tap a word to hear it, see the line in your own language and in IPA, then record.
  • Challenge — the same paragraph with less help each time, until you can say it unseen and then in your own words.

Shadow Studio lives in the app. Get the app

05 AI practice

Practise before the real conversation.

Talk for a minute about your project, your role or the update you owe someone. The report tells you how you came across, fixes what you got wrong, rebuilds your sentences and gives you better words. Then the coach reads it to you.

13 Questions

Before you download.

Is BE Mastery free?

Yes. The full twelve-week programme, Shadow Studio, the AI coach, Practice Partner and your progress are free today, with no card needed. A Premium plan is on its way; its price will be shown before anything is charged.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start straight away. Sign in with Apple, Google or email when you want your progress backed up — then it follows you between your iPhone and your Android phone.

What level do I need?

If you can read English but freeze when you speak, you are the learner it was built for. A short check at the start places you, and a gentler Foundations stage is there if you need it.

Can I use it in my own language?

The guidance, menus and help are available in 15 languages, including French, Spanish, Portuguese and Arabic. The practice itself is in English.

Is it on iPhone and Android?

Yes. BE Mastery is on the App Store and on Google Play. Sign in with the same account on both and your progress, words and reports come with you.

Who hears my recordings?

Your recordings are saved on your own device. To score a take, it is sent securely to our AI provider and not kept by us. Practice Partner live calls are never recorded.

Is Practice Partner safe?

It is for adults (18+), shows first names only, and has no photos or profiles. You can leave, block or report at any time.

Is there a programme for my trade?

Yes, for welding and the trades around it: welders, pipefitters, boilermakers, millwrights, refinery operators, instrumentation technicians, industrial electricians, HSE officers, NDT technicians and process engineers. Choose Welding English when you open the app.

Is the certificate an English test?

No. It shows you completed the programme, with your name on it. It is not a level test.
