How it works

Start small. Scale when the value is proven.

01

Quick Audit

A focused 60–75 minute session on one or two of your workflows. You get a prioritised 30-day roadmap and a simple ROI snapshot.

02

Quick win

We recommend the one automation with the best payoff for your business, and can set it up for you.

03

Scale what works

Add more automations only when the first one proves its value. You choose what to implement, with us or on your own.

The audit stands on its own. There is never an obligation to continue.

What the audit gives you

A 30-day roadmap you can act on.

An example for a small salon. Every business gets its own plan.

Week 1

Quick win: missed-call text-back

Every unanswered call triggers an instant text with a booking link. Set up, tested with staff phones, launched.

Week 2

Appointment reminders

A confirmation at booking, reminders 48 hours and 3 hours before, and an easy reschedule link.

Week 3

Review requests

Two hours after checkout, clients get a thank-you text with a direct Google review link. One gentle nudge for non-responders.

Week 4

Measure and tune

Review recovered leads, no-show rate and review count. Adjust timing and wording. Decide on phase 2.
