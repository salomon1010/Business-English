What the audit gives you

A 30-day roadmap you can act on.

An example for a small salon. Every business gets its own plan.

  1. Week 1

    Quick win: missed-call text-back. Every unanswered call triggers an instant text with a booking link. Set up, tested with staff phones, launched.

  2. Week 2

    Appointment reminders. A confirmation at booking, reminders 48 hours and 3 hours before, and an easy reschedule link. The front desk stops texting by hand.

  3. Week 3

    Review requests. Two hours after checkout, clients get a thank-you text with a direct Google review link. Non-responders get one gentle nudge.

  4. Week 4

    Measure and tune. Review recovered leads, no-show rate and review count. Adjust timing and wording. Decide on phase 2.
